About AnnelidaDB

This page contains information about the AnnelidaDB project, its goals, and the principal investigator.

Project Overview

AnnelidaDB is a dedicated database for annelid multi-omics resources, integrating genome, mitochondrial genome, transcriptome, single-cell, symbiont, and synteny data. Our goal is to support annelid research by making curated sequence and metadata easily discoverable and interoperable.

Principal Investigator

Prof. Jian-Wen Qiu (HKBU profile)
